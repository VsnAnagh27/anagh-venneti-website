I am an early-career researcher, having recently completed my PhD in Nuclear Astrophysics at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad. My doctoral research focused on neutron star structure, dense matter equations of state, and the application of Bayesian inference and computational methods to compact star physics. My broader research interests span nuclear astrophysics, multi-messenger astronomy, gravitational-wave observations, and the phenomenology of compact objects. Beyond these areas, I have a keen interest in exploring the theoretical foundations of gravitation and fundamental interactions, particularly General Relativity (GR), Quantum Field Theory (QFT), and approaches towards Quantum Gravity (QG). I am interested in understanding how these frameworks can inform our description of matter under extreme conditions, strong gravitational fields, and the physics of compact objects. I am currently seeking postdoctoral research opportunities and am keen to expand my research horizons through collaborations across nuclear theory, gravitational physics, and computational astrophysics. I welcome scientific discussions and opportunities to work with researchers and institutions exploring these interconnected areas.
