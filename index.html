Research Focus

Current Interests

Dense Matter EOS

Constraining the equation of state of dense nuclear matter from neutron star observations — mass, radius, and tidal deformability measurements.

Nuclear theory Meta-models Phase transitions

Gravitational Wave Astrophysics

Extracting nuclear physics information from gravitational wave signals of binary neutron star mergers detected by LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA.

GW signals BNS mergers Tidal deformability

Bayesian Inference

Developing MCMC and nested sampling pipelines for joint multi-messenger parameter estimation and equation of state inference.

MCMC Nested sampling Posterior analysis

Multi-Messenger Astrophysics

Combining gravitational wave, X-ray (NICER), and radio observations for simultaneous neutron star mass-radius-EOS constraints.

NICER Radio pulsars Joint analysis
